# Fernhill Goods: Returns, Exchanges & Refunds

*Fernhill Goods is a fictional retailer. This policy was written for a portfolio project.*

*Last updated by Customer Care. Holiday section added by Marketing. Refunds section reviewed by Finance.*

---

## The short version

We want you to love what you bought. If you don't, you can return most items within 30 days
for a refund. Fernhill Rewards members get even longer. Some exclusions apply (see below).

---

## 1. How long do I have?

You can return items within **30 days of delivery**.

## 2. Fernhill Rewards members

As a thank-you to our loyalty members, Fernhill Rewards members can return items within
**60 days**.

## 3. Holiday returns (added by Marketing)

Shopping early for the holidays? Items bought between **November 15 and December 31** can
be returned until **January 31**. Give gifts with confidence!

## 4. Final sale

Clearance items are **final sale** and cannot be returned or exchanged. Look for the
"Final Sale" tag on the product page. (Items in our regular seasonal sales are not final sale
unless they are marked as clearance.)

## 5. Defective or damaged items

If something arrives defective or breaks because of a manufacturing fault, you can return it
within **90 days**, even if it was marked final sale. We'll make it right.

## 6. How will I get my money back?

Refunds go back to your **original payment method**. Gift purchases are refunded as
**Fernhill store credit**.

## 7. What condition does it need to be in?

Items must be **unworn, with the original tags attached**. This doesn't apply to defective
items.

## 8. Large refunds (reviewed by Finance)

Refunds **over $500** need approval from a member of our team before they're issued. This
usually takes 1–2 business days.

## 9. Exchanges

We're happy to exchange an item for the **same item in a different size or color**, as long
as the one you want is **in stock**. For anything else, return the item and place a new order.

## 10. Rewards members and the holidays

Rewards members keep their 60-day window during the holidays, and the holiday extension
applies to everyone.

---

## FAQ

**Can I return something I've used?**
Not unless it's defective. Please keep the tags on until you're sure.

**I got a gift. Can I get cash back?**
Gifts are refunded as store credit, so the person who gave the gift isn't notified.

**Can I exchange my sweater for a different sweater?**
Exchanges are for a different size or color of the same item only.

**Does shipping get refunded?**
Original shipping charges are not refundable.
